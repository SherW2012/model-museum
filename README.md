# 模型博物馆

Static Three.js product museum. Start at dist/index.html through any HTTP static server.

## Collection
- iPhone 18 Pro Max: 96 meshes, official Apple AR exterior geometry.
- iPhone 18 Pro: 96 meshes, independently extracted smaller handset.
- Original Burgundy materials retained; Glacier, Silver and Black are explicitly identified digital recolorings.
- Drag / pinch / zoom, five camera presets, auto-rotation, reset, fullscreen, collection switching.

Reference photography: Apple product-viewer images referenced from https://www.apple.com/iphone-18-pro/
Conversion: scripts/convert_models.py; Blender bpy 4.5.3, GLB embedded textures and material extras. Original Apple assets remain attributable to Apple; this is an independent product design study.

## Technical decisions
Three.js 0.180.0 is self-hosted in dist/vendor. WebGL2, ACES filmic, pixel ratio capped at 1.75, RoomEnvironment/PMREM, hemisphere and two directional lights. No backend or account data. Add future devices in the exhibits registry in catalog.js with a model, title, and camera presets where needed.

## Validation
JavaScript syntax and local imports checked; both GLBs parsed, embedded textures and selectable body materials verified; exported Pro Max reopened and rendered offline in Blender to verify geometry and orientation. Live browser WebGL QA and WebMCP runtime validation unavailable in the current environment.

## Multi-brand collection (September 2026 update)
- Xiaomi 17 Ultra: 162.9 × 77.6 × 8.29 mm, 3 simulated finishes. Source: https://www.mi.com/global/product/xiaomi-17-ultra/specs/
- HUAWEI Pura 90 Pro Max: 164 × 77.1 × 8.1 mm, 5 simulated finishes including gradients. Source: https://consumer.huawei.com/cn/phones/pura90-pro-max/specs/
- Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra: 163.6 × 78.1 × 7.9 mm, 4 simulated finishes. Sources: https://www.samsungmobilepress.com/media-assets/galaxy-s26-ultra and https://news.samsung.com/global/samsung-unveils-galaxy-s26-series-the-most-intuitive-galaxy-ai-phone-yet

The three new GLBs are independently reconstructed exterior studies, generated by `scripts/build_flagships.py` in Blender 4.5.3 from manufacturer dimensions and reference photographs. They are not manufacturer CAD/AR files. Camera modules, lettering, hardware openings and optical internals are approximated. No internal mechanical accuracy is claimed. Clear provenance labels and manufacturer links accompany each exhibit. The Apple assets remain unchanged.

`dist/catalog.js` owns per-brand models, finishes, and sources. The viewer loads selected models on demand, caches them, ignores stale loading results, resets unsupported colors on model switches, and provides model-specific offline fallback renders. All model geometry is packaged locally; no external 3D asset endpoint is required. Front displays on reconstructed models are unlit black glass.
